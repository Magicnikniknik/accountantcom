In the Russian corporate sector, cybersecurity is strictly regulated by the state. The key objective is not only protection against external threats but also ensuring full compliance with the regulatory frameworks of FSTEC, FSB, and Roskomnadzor.

We act as your technical and legal guide, ensuring that your security architecture passes any regulatory inspection flawlessly.

## Echeloned Defense and GR Support

### 1. Secure Perimeter Design & Data Diodes
To protect industrial networks and CII, we implement physically unidirectional gateways.
* Using certified Data Diodes for the secure, compliant transmission of telemetry.
* Designing Personal Data Protection Systems (PDPA) of the highest security level.
* Implementing SIEM systems approved by local regulators.

### 2. Cryptography Integration (GOST)
Securing communication channels within the RF must be done using domestic algorithms.
* Building secure VPN tunnels (Site-to-Site) based on hardware certified by the FSB and FSTEC (ViPNet, Continent).
* Creating hybrid crypto-gateways that legally connect Russian standards with the investor's corporate protocols.

### 3. Government Relations & Regulatory Audits
You do not communicate directly with regulatory bodies. We handle it.
* Representing the company's interests during official inspections.
