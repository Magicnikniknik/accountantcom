## What to include in the first message

To scope the work without unnecessary back-and-forth, please include:

- legal form and country of ownership;
- whether the Russian operation is a legal entity, branch, representative office or another structure;
- current accounting system and reporting framework;
- whether headquarters reporting is required in addition to local records;
- approximate monthly document/transaction volume;
- relevant reporting period and deadlines;
- the specific problem you need to solve: recurring accounting, cleanup, RAS/IFRS reporting, tax-compliance workflow, due diligence or another finance task.

If the request concerns a transaction, historic accounting issue or reporting deadline, mention the date and material amounts where possible.

## Engagement boundary

An initial inquiry is used to define the facts and scope; it is not a professional opinion by itself. The engagement letter should identify the entities, period, deliverables, responsibilities and any assumptions.

Where a requested task requires a statutory audit, legal opinion or another regulated professional activity, the appropriately authorized provider must be identified explicitly for that scope. Accountant & Co. does not use this contact page to imply a license or regulated status that is not expressly stated for the specific engagement.

For general Russian tax-registration requirements, use the current Federal Tax Service of Russia as the authoritative public source. For IFRS requirements, use current material issued by the IFRS Foundation / IASB.
