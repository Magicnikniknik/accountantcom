Tax reporting depends on the quality of the accounting records beneath it. When tax-sensitive transactions are reviewed only at filing time, teams often have to reconstruct contracts, invoices, payment evidence and accounting logic under deadline pressure.

A better control model integrates tax-data preparation into the normal close while keeping final tax conclusions with the appropriate qualified reviewer.

## Identify tax-sensitive areas in advance

Maintain a register of balances and transaction types that require additional evidence or review. The exact population depends on the company, but the control should make missing documents and unresolved positions visible before the reporting deadline.

## Reconcile accounting and filing data

For each relevant return or calculation, keep a bridge from the underlying accounting accounts and schedules to the submitted figures. Differences between accounting and tax treatment should be documented rather than embedded in unexplained manual spreadsheets.

## Track open positions by period

An issue register should identify the transaction, amount, available evidence, unresolved question, responsible person and the reporting periods potentially affected. This is especially important when a conclusion cannot be finalized before the monthly close.

## Version calculations and source files

Retain the exact data set and calculation version used for a filing. If the ledger changes later, the team should be able to explain whether the filing was affected and what follow-up action was taken.

For operating support, see [Tax Compliance for Foreign-Owned Companies in Russia](/en/services/tax-compliance-foreign-owned-companies-russia/) and [Accounting Services in Russia](/en/services/accounting-services/).

Current tax obligations should be verified for the actual taxpayer, transaction and reporting period using the Federal Tax Service of Russia and other applicable official sources.
