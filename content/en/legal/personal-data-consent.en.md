# Consent to Process Personal Data for an Enquiry
_Effective 10 September 2026_

By ticking the separate checkbox beside the contact form, I freely, specifically, knowingly and unambiguously permit the owner and administrator of **accountantandco.com**, operating under the public-facing name **Accountant & Co.**, to process the **name, email address and message** I enter solely to:

- receive my enquiry;
- respond to it;
- discuss a possible scope of services before an engagement is entered into;
- retain relevant business correspondence where necessary for legitimate legal interests or mandatory obligations.

This consent **does not cover marketing newsletters, profiling, sale of personal information or sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising**.

The website form does not send the fields to a website database. It creates an email draft in my own email application, and nothing is sent until I confirm the email there.

I may withdraw consent for future consent-based processing by emailing **legal@accountantandco.com**. Withdrawal does not affect processing that had another lawful basis or was lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

I understand that a reply requires at least a return contact method and enough information to understand the enquiry. I should not send special-category data, passwords, bank-card details or sensitive documents by ordinary email unless a protected channel has been agreed.

See the [Privacy Policy](/en/legal/privacy-policy/) for more information about processing, retention and rights.
