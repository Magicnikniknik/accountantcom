Accountant & Co.

Accountant & Co. is an accounting and financial-compliance practice for businesses operating in Russia, including Russian companies with foreign ownership. The primary public service scope is bookkeeping and monthly close, tax-reporting coordination, RAS/IFRS reporting, financial review and due diligence, and cross-border finance support.

Typical client and deliverables

The service is relevant to Russian operating companies, foreign-owned Russian legal entities, subsidiaries and branches, overseas group-finance teams and businesses changing accounting providers or preparing for a transaction or external review.

Depending on scope, deliverables may include reconciled ledgers, monthly close schedules, tax/reporting calendars, management or headquarters reporting packs, RAS/IFRS adjustment schedules, issue logs, due-diligence findings and supporting evidence for external review.
