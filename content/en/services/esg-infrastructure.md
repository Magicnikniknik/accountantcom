Deploying data centers and High-Performance Computing (HPC) clusters in Russia requires unconditional adherence to digital sovereignty laws. Using foreign equipment involves mandatory legalization and integration with domestic security systems.

Accountant & Co. designs infrastructure solutions that ensure 100% compliance with local regulatory requirements and global corporate standards.

## Architecture of Legal Deployment

### 1. Data Localization (152-FZ) & CII Protection (187-FZ)
We bring your technological assets into full compliance with federal laws.
* Categorization of Critical Information Infrastructure (CII) facilities.
* Designing Demilitarized Zones (DMZ) to isolate industrial segments.
* Ensuring the legal processing of RF citizens' personal data strictly within the country.

### 2. Hardware Certification & Legalization
The import and operation of telecommunications equipment require coordination with authorized agencies.
* Managing the process of obtaining FSB notifications for cryptographic devices.
* Securing operational permits from the Ministry of Industry and Trade.
* Integrating imported computational capacities with certified domestic security solutions.

### 3. ESG Compliance in RF Realities
We adapt your international environmental responsibility standards (GRI) to the Russian legal framework.
* Converting energy consumption metrics into legally binding reports valid for specialized RF ministries.
* Securing official "green" project status in the RF to access legal tax incentives.
