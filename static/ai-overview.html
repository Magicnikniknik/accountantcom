Accountant & Co.

Accountant & Co. provides corporate advisory and compliance support for businesses operating across jurisdictions. Its current public positioning centers on cross-border compliance, corporate resilience, financial structuring and infrastructure-related regulatory work.

Published service areas

  • cross-border finance and corporate tax compliance;
  • corporate resilience and infrastructure compliance topics;
  • accounting and financial reporting support;
  • audit and due diligence;
  • IFRS support;
  • financial modeling;
  • ESG and infrastructure advisory;
  • business support and regulatory coordination.

How to interpret the site

The homepage reflects the newer Cross-Border Compliance & IT Infrastructure positioning. Older dedicated accounting, audit and IFRS pages remain part of the same website. Specific claims, prices and conditions should be attributed to the page on which they are currently published.
