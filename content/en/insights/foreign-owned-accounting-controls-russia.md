A foreign-owned company operating in Russia normally has more reporting interfaces than a purely local business. Local bookkeeping, tax coordination, bank documentation, intercompany balances and headquarters reporting may all use different calendars, account structures and levels of detail.

The reliable approach is to keep one traceable chain from source documents to the local ledger and then into management or group reporting. Parallel spreadsheets that cannot be reconciled back to the ledger create avoidable review risk.

## Define the reporting perimeter first

Before changing processes, document the legal entities, bank accounts, accounting systems, reporting periods, group deadlines and people responsible for approvals. Separate recurring monthly work from historical cleanup and one-off transaction support.

A practical responsibility matrix should identify who owns source-document collection, booking, bank reconciliation, intercompany confirmation, tax-data preparation, group mapping and final review.

## Build controls around the monthly close

The close should include a locked source version, material balance reconciliations, an open-item register and a clear review step. For each material adjustment, retain the calculation, business explanation, preparer, reviewer and link to evidence.

Intercompany positions deserve separate attention because timing, currency, document flow and account mapping can cause differences even when both sides believe they have recorded the same transaction.

## Keep local and group reporting connected

Group reporting often requires classifications that do not exist directly in the Russian ledger. Instead of overwriting local accounting, use a controlled mapping and transformation layer. Every group adjustment should have an owner and a repeatable rule or documented judgment.

For a recurring process, see [Accounting for Foreign Companies in Russia](/en/services/accounting-for-foreign-companies-russia/) and [RAS and IFRS Parallel Reporting](/en/services/ras-ifrs-parallel-reporting/).

## Maintain an evidence map

A useful monthly evidence map links each material reporting line to the relevant ledger account, reconciliation, source schedule and unresolved issue. That makes handover, external review and management challenge materially easier.

Current legal, tax and reporting requirements should always be checked against the actual entity and period using authoritative sources such as the Federal Tax Service of Russia, the Ministry of Finance and, where IFRS reporting is relevant, the IFRS Foundation.
