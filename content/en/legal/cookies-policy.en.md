# Cookies Policy
_Updated: 10 September 2026_

This Policy describes only technologies actually used, or capable of being loaded, on **accountantandco.com**.

## 1. What works without consent

The website may use strictly necessary technical mechanisms for page delivery, security and remembering your privacy choice. In particular, `cookie_consent_status` stores only “granted” or “denied”. It may be kept in localStorage and, as a fallback, a first-party cookie for up to 12 months.

## 2. What loads only after consent

Before you press “Allow analytics”, the website does not load:

- Google Tag Manager `GTM-PSQ6T2B9`;
- the optional analytics script at `a.antonov.app`.

Advertising cookies and personalised advertising are not required for the website. If the analytics configuration changes in the future, this Policy should be updated before new purposes or providers are enabled.

## 3. Refusing optional technologies

“Necessary only” keeps optional analytics disabled. Refusal does not block access to the website's main content.

**Global Privacy Control (GPC)** and **Do Not Track (DNT)** signals are treated as a refusal to load optional analytics.

## 4. Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time through **“Cookie settings”** in the footer. Reopening the banner lets you permit or refuse optional services again. Withdrawal applies prospectively and does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

## 5. Yandex Maps

The map on the contact page is not loaded automatically. It connects to Yandex only after you separately press “Load map”. Yandex may then use its own technical storage or receive technical data. If you do not want that connection, do not load the map.

## 6. Browser controls

Your browser can delete cookies and localStorage. After deletion, the website will ask for your choice again. Blocking all cookies may prevent preferences from being remembered but the core content remains available.

## 7. Contact

Questions about cookies or withdrawal of consent: **legal@accountantandco.com**.
