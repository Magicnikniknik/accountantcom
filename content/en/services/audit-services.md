Accountant & Co. supports companies that need an independent-looking **financial review process, audit preparation or due diligence**, but this page deliberately distinguishes those advisory services from a statutory audit opinion.

A useful review should answer specific questions: are the reported numbers supported by the ledger and source documents, are material balances reconciled, are there unusual transactions or accounting-policy issues, and what information would an external auditor, investor, lender or buyer likely request next?

## Financial review

A financial review can include:

- trial-balance and ledger analysis;
- reconciliation of bank, receivables, payables and intercompany balances;
- review of unusual or long-outstanding balances;
- review of revenue and expense cut-off;
- analysis of material manual adjustments;
- consistency checks between accounting records and management reporting;
- review of supporting schedules for major balance-sheet items;
- an issues list with priority, evidence status and next action.

The output is normally a findings document and supporting schedules, not an audit opinion.

## Audit preparation

If a company is preparing for an external or statutory audit, we can help organize the accounting side before the auditor starts:

1. close and reconcile material accounts;
2. collect lead schedules and supporting documents;
3. explain material movements and manual adjustments;
4. prepare intercompany confirmations and reconciliations;
5. maintain a request tracker;
6. resolve accounting issues that can be corrected before fieldwork;
7. hand the auditor a consistent evidence package.

This can reduce time lost to basic document retrieval and unexplained differences.

## Financial due diligence

For acquisitions, investments, refinancing or shareholder decisions, the work can focus on:

- quality of earnings and non-recurring items;
- revenue concentration and unusual revenue recognition patterns;
- normalized EBITDA or other agreed performance measures;
- working-capital trends and seasonality;
- debt, cash and debt-like items;
- overdue receivables and payables;
- related-party and intercompany balances;
- tax and accounting issues visible in the data;
- reliability and completeness of the source information.

See the dedicated [Financial Due Diligence in Russia](/en/services/financial-due-diligence-russia/) page for the transaction-focused workflow.

## What this page does not claim

**A statutory audit is a regulated engagement and is not the same thing as an internal financial review, audit preparation or due diligence.** Where a client requires a statutory audit opinion or another regulated assurance report, the engagement must expressly identify the appropriately authorized audit provider and the applicable professional standards.

Accountant & Co. does not use the phrase “audit services” on this page to imply a statutory-auditor license that is not explicitly stated for the engagement.

## Information required to scope a review

Please provide:

- legal entity and reporting period;
- purpose of the review;
- accounting framework and accounting system;
- trial balance and general ledger availability;
- number of entities and locations;
- whether intercompany balances are material;
- available tax registers and reconciliations;
- expected deliverable and deadline;
- whether an external auditor, lender, investor or buyer will use the output.

## Source boundary

For current Russian tax requirements, use the Federal Tax Service of Russia as the authoritative public source. For IFRS reporting requirements, use current material from the IFRS Foundation / IASB. A review conclusion must be based on the actual records and period under review, not generic website text.
