Cross-border transactions become difficult when the contract, invoice, accounting entry, payment purpose and group reporting description tell different stories. Accountant & Co. helps finance teams build a consistent evidence chain around transactions involving a Russian operation.

The objective is practical: make the commercial substance, accounting treatment, approvals and supporting documents traceable before a bank, auditor, tax adviser or headquarters reviewer asks for them.

## What the service can include

- transaction and document mapping;
- accounting and payment-data reconciliation;
- support-file preparation for bank or currency-control questions;
- intercompany balance and invoice reconciliation;
- coordination of group reporting inputs for cross-border transactions;
- tracking contracts, invoices, acceptance documents and payments;
- issue logs for missing evidence or inconsistent transaction descriptions;
- preparation of finance data for tax, legal, customs or sanctions specialists.

## A documented transaction chain

A controlled file normally connects six elements:

1. the commercial agreement and approved transaction purpose;
2. the invoice, acceptance, delivery or other performance evidence;
3. the payment instruction and bank records;
4. the local accounting entry and tax register, where relevant;
5. the intercompany counter-entry or group reporting treatment;
6. questions, decisions and additional documents requested during review.

This does not eliminate external review. It makes the underlying facts easier to present consistently and gaps easier to identify early.

## Intercompany and headquarters coordination

International groups often record the same transaction differently because of timing, foreign exchange, account mapping or incomplete documentation. A recurring process can match invoices and payments, identify timing differences, assign owners to unresolved items and confirm the final balance used for group reporting.

Where local records and group reporting use different frameworks, the transaction can also be included in the documented [RAS-to-IFRS reporting bridge](/en/services/ras-ifrs-parallel-reporting/).

## What this service does not promise

Accountant & Co. does not guarantee that a bank, authority, counterparty or service provider will accept a transaction, execute a payment or keep a particular route available. Requirements can change and depend on the parties, banks, jurisdictions, goods or services and facts of the transaction.

This finance-support scope is not a substitute for a legal, sanctions, customs, transfer-pricing or regulated banking opinion. When one is required, the relevant specialist should be engaged explicitly and provided with an organized factual record.

## Information needed for an initial scope

Please provide the entities and jurisdictions involved, transaction purpose, currency, expected timing, relevant contracts and invoices, current payment status, accounting treatment and any questions already received from a bank or reviewer. Sensitive documents can be requested after the engagement channel and confidentiality arrangements are agreed.
